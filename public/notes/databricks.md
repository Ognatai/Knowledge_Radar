### TL;DR

Databricks is a data and AI company headquartered in San Francisco, founded in 2013 by the original creators of the lakehouse architecture and of the open-source projects Apache Spark, Delta Lake, [[mlflow|MLflow]] and Unity Catalog (Databricks, n.d.). Its mission is "to simplify and democratize data and AI", and more than 20,000 organisations, including 70% of the Fortune 500, use its Data + AI Platform (Databricks, n.d.). In this knowledge base it appears as the origin of the [[data-lakehouse|data lakehouse]] architecture, as a company founded by the creators of [[apache-spark|Apache Spark]] and as an example of a governance layer with Unity Catalog ([[data-governance|data governance]]).

### Mandate and tasks

Databricks states that it is "on a mission to simplify and democratize data and AI, helping data and AI teams solve the world's toughest problems" (Databricks, n.d.). Its Data + AI Platform lets organisations take control of their data and use it with AI: anyone can discover and use data with automation and natural language, and technical teams can build and deploy secure data and AI applications (Databricks, n.d.).

### Structure and working methods

The company has its origins in academia and the open-source community (Databricks, n.d.). Spark began at UC Berkeley around resilient distributed datasets ([[apache-spark|Apache Spark]]), and Databricks researchers, among them Michael Armbrust, Ali Ghodsi, Reynold Xin and [[matei-zaharia|Matei Zaharia]], set out the lakehouse architecture at CIDR 2021 after Databricks had begun developing Delta Lake in 2016 ([[data-lakehouse|data lakehouse]]). It works with a network of more than 1,200 cloud, ISV and consulting partners (Databricks, n.d.).

### Relevance for AI development

Databricks links data engineering and machine learning on one platform: data stored in open table formats in a lakehouse can be processed with Spark, governed with Unity Catalog, including lineage and permissions on tables and models, and used to train and track models with MLflow ([[data-lakehouse|data lakehouse]], [[data-governance|data governance]], [[experiment-tracking|experiment tracking]]). For organisations that keep their data there, feature pipelines and model training can run in the same environment ([[ml-pipelines|ML pipelines]]).

### Auftrag und Aufgaben

Seine Data + AI Platform soll Organisationen die Kontrolle über ihre Daten geben und sie mit KI nutzbar machen: Alle Beschäftigten sollen Daten mit Automatisierung und natürlicher Sprache finden und nutzen können, und technische Teams sollen sichere Daten- und KI-Anwendungen bauen und bereitstellen können (Databricks, n.d.).
