### TL;DR

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company, founded in 2015 as a nonprofit with the mission "to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity" (OpenAI, n.d.-a). Since a restructuring announced on 28 October 2025, the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation controls the for-profit OpenAI Group PBC, a public benefit corporation that must advance the mission and consider the interests of all stakeholders (OpenAI, n.d.-a). OpenAI develops the [[gpt|GPT]] model family and [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]], introduced in November 2022, and offers its models through ChatGPT, Codex and an API platform; its Charter commits it to broadly distributed benefits, long-term safety, technical leadership and a cooperative orientation (OpenAI, n.d.-b; OpenAI, n.d.-c).

### Mandate and tasks

OpenAI describes itself as "an AI research and deployment company"; its mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity, and it will "consider our mission fulfilled if our work aids others to achieve this outcome" (OpenAI, n.d.-c). The Charter defines AGI as "highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work" and states that its primary fiduciary duty is to humanity (OpenAI, n.d.-b). It commits OpenAI to use any influence over AGI's deployment for the benefit of all, to do and spread safety research, to stay at the cutting edge of capabilities because policy and safety advocacy alone would be insufficient, and to cooperate with other research and policy institutions (OpenAI, n.d.-b).

### Structure and working methods

OpenAI was founded as a nonprofit in 2015 and created a for-profit subsidiary in 2019 to scale research and deployment, governed and controlled by the nonprofit (OpenAI, n.d.-a). In the structure announced on 28 October 2025, the nonprofit became the OpenAI Foundation and the for-profit became OpenAI Group PBC; the Foundation continues to control the Group and holds conventional equity in it, and both have the same mission (OpenAI, n.d.-a). The Charter commits OpenAI to stop competing with and start assisting a value-aligned, safety-conscious project that comes close to building AGI first, and expects that safety and security concerns will reduce traditional publishing while increasing the sharing of safety, policy and standards research (OpenAI, n.d.-b).

### Key outputs

- Products and developer offerings: ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Education, Codex, the API platform, an Apps SDK and open models (OpenAI, n.d.-c); OpenAI models are also available through cloud platforms ([[cloud-ai-platforms|cloud AI platforms]]).

### Relevance for AI development

OpenAI's GPT models and ChatGPT made large language models a mass product and set many conventions for chat interfaces, instruction following and RLHF-based alignment ([[large-language-models|large language models]], [[llm-alignment|LLM alignment]]). For developers, its API and SDKs are common building blocks for LLM applications and agents ([[agent-frameworks|agent frameworks]]). Its models are also the subject of legal questions, for example the Munich Regional Court's ruling on song lyrics reproduced by ChatGPT ([[copyright-and-ai-training-data|copyright and AI training data]]); as a provider of general-purpose AI models in the EU it falls under the [[eu-ai-act|AI Act]] and the [[ai-office|AI Office]].

### Auftrag und Aufgaben

OpenAI beschreibt sich als „an AI research and deployment company“; seine Mission ist, dass allgemeine künstliche Intelligenz der gesamten Menschheit nützt, und es würde die Mission als erfüllt ansehen, wenn seine Arbeit anderen hilft, dieses Ziel zu erreichen (OpenAI, n.d.-c). Die Charta definiert AGI als „highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work“ und erklärt, die vorrangige treuhänderische Pflicht gelte der Menschheit (OpenAI, n.d.-b). Sie verpflichtet OpenAI, jeden Einfluss auf den Einsatz von AGI zum Nutzen aller einzusetzen, Sicherheitsforschung zu betreiben und zu verbreiten, an der Spitze der Fähigkeiten zu bleiben, weil politische und sicherheitsbezogene Fürsprache allein nicht genüge, und mit anderen Forschungs- und Politikeinrichtungen zusammenzuarbeiten (OpenAI, n.d.-b).

### Aufbau und Arbeitsweise

OpenAI wurde 2015 als gemeinnützige Organisation gegründet und schuf 2019 eine gewinnorientierte Tochter, um Forschung und Bereitstellung zu skalieren, gesteuert und kontrolliert von der gemeinnützigen Organisation (OpenAI, n.d.-a). In der am 28. Oktober 2025 angekündigten Struktur wurde die gemeinnützige Organisation zur OpenAI Foundation und die gewinnorientierte Gesellschaft zur OpenAI Group PBC; die Foundation kontrolliert die Group weiterhin, hält reguläres Eigenkapital an ihr, und beide verfolgen dieselbe Mission (OpenAI, n.d.-a). Die Charta verpflichtet OpenAI, den Wettbewerb mit einem wertekonformen, sicherheitsbewussten Projekt einzustellen und es zu unterstützen, wenn dieses dem Bau von AGI zuerst nahekommt, und erwartet, dass Sicherheitsbedenken klassisches Veröffentlichen verringern, während das Teilen von Forschung zu Sicherheit, Politik und Standards wichtiger wird (OpenAI, n.d.-b).
